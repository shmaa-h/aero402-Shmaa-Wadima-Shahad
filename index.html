Engineering Model & Numerical Details

Adopted model

Steady, two-dimensional, inviscid flow of a calorically perfect gas. Shock–expansion theory, weak attached shocks, Prandtl–Meyer expansions, and vector panel pressure forces. Constant γ for each analysis.

Supported inputs

M∞: 1.5–5.0
α: −10° to +10°
δL, δT: 2°–15°
p∞ > 0 Pa; c > 0 m; γ > 1
Current γ: 1.4000

Numerical controls

Root tolerance: 10⁻¹⁰ rad
Maximum iterations: 200
Zero-turn tolerance: 10⁻⁹ rad
Near-zero denominator tolerance: 10⁻¹⁴
Near-zero CD tolerance: 10⁻¹⁰

Body coordinates: +x from leading to trailing edge; +y upward; positive angles counterclockwise. φ∞ = +α. Internally, angles are radians, pressures are Pa, and lengths are m.

Warning threshold: a compression within 1° below θmax. At equality, the limiting attached root is accepted with a warning; any excess is an error. Expansion requires νtarget < νmax. The existing numerical inversion bracket is 1 ≤ M ≤ 100, separate from the supported freestream range; a target beyond this bracket produces an explicit numerical error.

Errors block dependent results. Warnings preserve otherwise valid calculations. No values are silently clamped, and unavailable states are not replaced by freestream values or zeros.

Engineering model & equation traceability

Team specification: steady, two-dimensional, inviscid flow of a calorically perfect gas with constant γ; a sharp diamond wedge; supersonic freestream; shock–expansion theory; pressure forces only. Viscous and skin-friction drag are neglected.

Body coordinates: +x from leading edge to trailing edge, +y upward, positive angles counterclockwise. Keep φ∞ = +α; UF = +δL, UR = −δT, LF = −δL, LR = +δT. The upper and lower sequences are independent; each rear panel receives its front panel's downstream state. Positive upper turns and negative lower turns are compression.
